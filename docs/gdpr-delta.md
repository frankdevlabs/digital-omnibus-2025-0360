# GDPR delta sheet — current text vs the Omnibus, per article

The controller-facing view of file **2025/0360 (COD)**: for each GDPR article the Digital Omnibus touches,
**what you must do today**, **what the Commission proposed**, **where the Council landed**, **what actually
changes for a controller**, and **whether it is settled**.

This is the counterpart to [`what-changed.md`](what-changed.md), which is organised by *institution* and by
*what moved vs earlier reporting*. This page is organised by *GDPR article* — one row, one screen, for the
question a corporate lawyer actually asks.

**How to read a row.** The **Article** cell links to the full analysis in [`provisions/`](provisions/). The
**Today** cell states the obligation as it stands in the GDPR in force and deep-links the article in the
AVG Explorer (`gdpr.mrfrank.dev`, Dutch text of Regulation (EU) 2016/679). The **Commission** and **Council**
cells deep-link the operative text in [`../extracts/commission/`](../extracts/commission/) and
[`../extracts/council/`](../extracts/council/) — **every substantive claim on this page is derived from those
committed extracts**, not from summaries or reporting.

**Two Council texts are cited.** The named baseline is **ST 9547/26** (21 May 2026), as in
[`what-changed.md`](what-changed.md). The Council's **negotiating mandate is ST 10729/26** (22 June 2026), so
each Council cell carries the **Δ ST 10729/26** delta after the baseline — that delta is what changes Arts 49,
88b and 37(7). Where a text does not address a provision the cell says so (e.g. *"not addressed in
ST 9547/26"*) rather than guessing.

**Status legend** (a three-value reading imposed for this sheet; the underlying institutional detail is in
[`../data/positions.csv`](../data/positions.csv) and [`institutional-positions.md`](institutional-positions.md)):

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **agreed** | Survives in the latest Council text and no institution has tabled opposition — plan for it. |
| **contested** | Institutions diverge, or the drafting is still moving between compromises — track it. |
| **dropped** | The Commission's amendment is gone from the latest Council text (deleted, or relocated out of the GDPR) — current law stands, or the rule moves elsewhere. |

Nothing here is settled law: the file is pre-trilogue and the Parliament's lead joint ITRE/LIBE report is the
missing third leg. **Not legal advice** ([`../DISCLAIMER.md`](../DISCLAIMER.md)).

[cyber single-entry point](provisions/cyber-single-entry-point.md) (NIS2 Art 23a/23b and the sectoral
reporting duties), the [EUDPR mirror](provisions/eudpr-mirror.md) for EU institutions, and the
[repeals and P2B](provisions/final-repeals-p2b.md) strand — have their own provision pages. The Parliament
column is deliberately absent: no lead joint ITRE/LIBE report has been adopted, so committee positions are
tracked per provision in [`what-changed.md`](what-changed.md) and
[`institutional-positions.md`](institutional-positions.md) rather than turned into a controller instruction.
