## Assessment

**In scope and substantive** — a real EP procedural milestone (the single largest tabling of amendments
on the file so far). Scope is unambiguous: OEIL was queried on `2025/0360(COD)`; the amendment PDF cover
carries `2025/0360(COD)` / `COM(2025)0837` and names the correct rapporteurs — **not** the AI-Act file
2025/0359. (General web search conflates the two omnibus files; disregarded.)

Per the upgrade rule this runs on the **parliament-text** playbook, but on its **metadata-only** branch:
PE791.883 (amendments **1741–1840**) is only **one part** of a large multi-part set — the full tabling
runs to **more than 1,762 amendments** (and at least to no. 1840), split across ~18 PE-numbered doceo
documents. The committee **documents listing feeds** that would enumerate every part are still
AWS-WAF-gated (health #118), so
the individual PE numbers can't yet be fully enumerated. Transcribing ~1,800 amendments across ~18 PDFs
is far beyond a single triage run (cf. #102 = 228 amendments in one doc, itself a full transcription).

**Therefore: register the tabled-amendment set metadata-only, make no per-provision claims, and set the
`pending_operative_text` follow-up trigger** so the transcription re-surfaces. These are competing MEP
amendments, not an adopted committee line (the joint committee vote is expected ~Q4 2026), so they are
**not** folded into the per-provision comparison cells.

## Outcome — the confirmed part is transcribed after all (2026-08-05)

The metadata-only branch was the right call for the **set**, but not for the part actually in hand.
`fetch_blocked_doc.py` had already delivered `CJ72-AM-791883_EN.pdf` (302,775 B, 60 pp) past the WAF, and
the guide's rule is that metadata-only registration is licensed by *non-retrievability*, not by the size
of the wider set. Precedent: `JURI-AM-790236` (228 amendments) was committed and transcribed. So before
merging:

- the PDF was committed as `sources/parliament/CJ72-AM-791883_amendments-1741-1840_2026-07-27.pdf`, and
  the `data/documents.yaml` entry gained the `local:` field (the `access: cite-only` marker was dropped —
  the file is in the repo);
- `sources/README.md` gained the `[PDF]` link every other EP row carries;
- **amendments 1741–1840 were transcribed in full** into
  `extracts/parliament/CJ72-AM-791883_amendments-1741-1840.md` — 100 amendments, 51 tablers, per-amendment
  `<a id="amendment-N">` anchors, grouped by the proposal article they touch (Art 6 NIS2 Art 23a
  single-entry point 1741–1810 · Art 7 eIDAS 1811–1814 · Art 8 DORA 1815–1818 · Art 9 CER 1819–1821 ·
  Art 9a new AI Act/CRA 1822–1823 · Art 10 repeals 1824–1840);
- `STATUS.md` and `docs/institutional-positions.md` were re-worded from "metadata-only" to "one part
  transcribed, remaining parts pending".

`pending_operative_text: true` **stays** — it now covers the un-enumerated remainder of the ~1,800
amendments, not the part in hand. Steps 1–5 of the plan above are otherwise unchanged.

Two substantive readings the transcription supports (previously not assertable): on the NIS2 single-entry
point, **14** amendments replace the ENISA-run point with **national** entry points; on the repeal
article, **14 of 17** amendments strike a repeal — **13** of them the **P2B Regulation (EU) 2019/1150** —
which is consistent with, and now evidenced for, the draft report's own restoration of P2B.
