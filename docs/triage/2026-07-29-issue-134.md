## Assessment

**In scope and substantive — but a procedural milestone, not a content position.**

- **Scope:** the block's own cover carries interinstitutional reference **2025/0360(COD)** and the joint
  ITRE/LIBE draft report by Salla & Kaljurand — the data/cyber Digital Omnibus, **not** the AI-Act file
  2025/0359. Confirmed.
- **Nature:** these are **competing MEP tablings**, not the rapporteurs' text and **not** an adopted
  committee position (the joint ITRE/LIBE committee vote is expected ~Q4 2026). No per-provision claim can
  or should be made from a count alone.
- **Retrievability vs scale:** the operative text **is** retrievable (the fetcher works despite the
  europarl documents-feed outage, health #118 — that outage hits the `T1-05`/`T1-21` listing feeds, not the
  doceo document endpoint). The reason to defer is **scale**: transcribing >1,760 amendments across many
  blocks is a dedicated `transcribe-parliament-extract` workstream, far larger than the 78/124/228-amendment
  single-document passes (#88/#61/#102), and out of scope for one procedural-confirmation issue.

Recommendation: **integrate the milestone** (STATUS + TIMELINE), **register the umbrella document
metadata-only** with `pending_operative_text` so the (large) transcription re-surfaces, and set a
**Next-milestones** follow-up. Make **no** per-amendment content claims.
