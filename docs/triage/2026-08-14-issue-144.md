## What was flagged
A Bluesky post (indexed 2026-08-13) reports that the German Federal Data Protection Commissioner
(**BfDI**) issued a report on cookie banners recommending the **Digital Omnibus** as the vehicle to
anchor **binding, machine-readable data-protection preferences and consent-management services**.

**Confirmed at the primary source.** The BfDI press release (PM 12/2026,
`bfdi.bund.de/…/12_Cookie-Banner.html`) links a report — **"BfDI-Datenbarometer: Befragungsergebnisse
und Empfehlungen — Cookie-Manager"**, August 2026, subtitle *"Weg mit den Cookie-Bannern!
Cookie-Manager praxistauglich und rechtssicher im EU Digital Omnibus verankern"* — obtained (not
WAF-blocked) and committed under `sources/advisory/`. Verified verbatim in the report body:
- *"Deutschland sollte den Prozess um den EU Digital Omnibus nutzen, um EU-weit starke Regeln für
  Cookie-Manager zu verankern."*
- *"Einwilligungssignale rechtlich verbindlich machen … Einwilligungssignale müssen für
  Website-Betreibende abschließend und verbindlich sein. Zusätzliche Banner sollten entfallen."*
- Points to Germany's **Einwilligungsverwaltungsverordnung (EinwV**, in force since 1 Apr 2025) as a
  pilot model to raise to EU level; and urges strengthening the vetting/recognition role of
  independent authorities EU-wide. Survey findings: ~⅔ would use a central consent-management
  service; ~8 in 10 want settings to apply across all websites.

**Faithfulness note.** The report does **not** cite "GDPR Art 88b" or the word *maschinenlesbar* by
number/term. It argues for **legally binding, once-set, cross-site consent signals and recognised
consent-management services** anchored via the Omnibus — which is the **substance** of the
Commission's Art 88b machine-readable-consent element. The Art 88b mapping below is this repo's
analysis, not a citation in the BfDI text.

## Assessment
**In scope (2025/0360, data/cyber — ePrivacy Art 5(3) / GDPR Art 88a-88b cookie strand).** Squarely the
cookie-consent strand of COM(2025) 837; not the AI-Act file 2025/0359.

**Substantive, and analytically notable.** This is an official **national supervisory authority**
positioning on how the Digital Omnibus should handle cookie consent — a primary document, not
reportage. It backs the once-set, binding consent signal (the Art 88b element the **Council deleted**
in **ST 10729/26**, 22 Jun — see [what-changed](../what-changed.md#st-1072926--the-22-june-negotiating-mandate-δ-vs-st-954726)).
Crucially it places the German **federal DPA opposite the German _government_'s Council line**
(Germany pressed to *delete* Art 88b per WK 3736/26 and the noyb/Politico reports — see
[member-state-positions](../member-state-positions.md) and [fault-line #3](../fault-lines.md)),
alongside IMCO, EDRi/noyb and German civil society (vzbv, Digitale Gesellschaft). Distinct from the
noyb #KillTheCookieBanner items (#125/#128): an independent authority's evidence-backed
recommendation, not an NGO campaign.
