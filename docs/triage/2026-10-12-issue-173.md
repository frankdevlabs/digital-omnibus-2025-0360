## Assessment
**In scope.** The ranking is explicitly about the GDPR + ePrivacy strand of the Digital Omnibus
(2025/0360), not the AI-Act file 2025/0359.

**Substantive, but advocacy — not a primary institutional document.** The upgrade rule (a Tier-2/3 item
surfacing a primary institutional document) does **not** fire here: gdpr-omnibus.eu is noyb's *derived*
scoring of already-tabled amendments, not itself a committee opinion/report to transcribe. So it stays on
the `stakeholder-social` playbook — record noyb's position/analysis, make no per-amendment repo claims
from an advocacy score.

It is nonetheless a useful signal on the **Parliament's trajectory** ahead of the still-pending joint
ITRE/LIBE draft report, and corroborates the civil-society read already in the repo (EPP weakening /
Greens–S&D–Left–Renew defending).
