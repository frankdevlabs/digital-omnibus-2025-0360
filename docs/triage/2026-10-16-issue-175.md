  check** that refuses invoice/instalment ("pay-later") payment is a solely-automated decision caught by
  **GDPR Art 22**.
- **Point 62 (the in-scope hook):** the AG records that the Commission "has submitted a proposal for a
  regulation … which … aims to amend the GDPR … and which would eliminate any doubt as to the interpretation
  of the term 'decision' within the meaning of Article 22(2)(a) GDPR, since the regulation would then
  expressly not require that a decision be based solely on automated processing." She **declines to give it
  interpretive weight** because it "has not yet entered into force and has not even been approved by the
  Parliament and the Council in its current form."
- **Points 63–67 (substance):** Art 22(2)(a) read restrictively — a solely-automated decision is "necessary"
  for a contract only where **no equally effective, less-intrusive alternative** (e.g. human review) exists;
  the **controller bears the burden**; time/cost savings alone do not suffice.

## Assessment
**In scope — 2025/0360, not the AI-Act file.** The proposal the AG describes (amend the GDPR to drop the
"solely" requirement from the Art 22(2)(a) derogation) is the data/cyber **Digital Omnibus — COM(2025) 837,
2025/0360(COD)**. The AI-Act omnibus (2025/0359) does **not** amend GDPR Art 22. The clause the AG
paraphrases is exactly the Commission's *"regardless of whether the decision could be taken otherwise than
by solely automated means"* wording tracked on
[`provisions/gdpr-art22-automated-decisions.md`](../provisions/gdpr-art22-automated-decisions.md) — the only
pending proposal amending Art 22. Scope confirmed.

**Substantive, and an upgrade.** This is the **#47 pattern**: a Tier-3 Bluesky post surfaces a **primary
document** — a CJEU AG Opinion that (a) interprets the exact provision this file would amend and (b)
expressly references this file. So this is treated as a jurisprudential development, not mere social
commentary. Per repo convention, CJEU cases are cited **inline by number/name** (cf. C-804/25 on
[`provisions/gdpr-art49-third-country-tax-transfers.md`](../provisions/gdpr-art49-third-country-tax-transfers.md),
and *SCHUFA* C-634/21), **not** registered in `data/documents.yaml`; the commentator (who merely shared a
court screenshot) is **not** a position actor and gets **no** `docs/stakeholders.md` entry.

**Analytical significance.** The AG's restrictive reading aligns with the **EDPB–EDPS** Joint Opinion 2/2026
(necessity = no less-intrusive means) and **cuts against** the Commission's permissive rewrite — the same
rewrite the **Council already deleted** in ST 9547/26. An AG Opinion is non-binding; the Court's judgment is
pending.
