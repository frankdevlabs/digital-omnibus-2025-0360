## How to read this file

Each amendment is transcribed **as tabled**, in the document's own order and numbering (329–532), with the
two-column *Text proposed by the Commission* / *Amendment* pair reproduced and the tabling MEP(s) named.
This is a compilation of **competing** MEP proposals — several amendments target the **same** recital or
article in opposite directions, so they cannot all be adopted. Where a MEP strikes a Commission
amending-point, the *Amendment* column reads *deleted* and the **Action** is "Deleted" (the omnibus change
is removed and the underlying instrument kept); a "New" action adds a recital/article/point not in the
Commission text. Per-amendment *Justifications* are transcribed where the tabler supplied one, in their
original language. Column text is reproduced faithfully from the PDF text layer; the source's own
superscript footnote markers are rendered inline.

## Article 2 of the proposal — amendments to the Single Digital Gateway (Regulation (EU) 2018/1724)

### Amendment 342 — Article 2 a (new)
<a id="amendment-342"></a>

- **Author:** Morten Løkkegaard, Svenja Hahn, Jeannette Baljeu, Sandro Gozi
- **Target:** Article 2 a (new)
- **Action:** New

**Text proposed by the Commission**

*(none — new provision)*

**Amendment**

Article2a Member States shall ensure that the procedures referred to in this entry are accessible and can be completed fully online through the Single Digital Gateway, including by cross-border users and businesses established in another Member State, without requiring physical presence, national establishment or the use of national-only identification means, unless such requirement is objectively justified, proportionate and provided for by Union law.

### Amendment 528 — Article 10 – paragraph 1
<a id="amendment-528"></a>

- **Author:** Sophia Kircher
- **Target:** Article 10 – paragraph 1
- **Action:** Amended

**Text proposed by the Commission**

1.     Regulation 2019/1150/EU is repealed with effect from [date = entry into application of this Regulation].

**Amendment**

1.    Regulation 2019/1150/EU is amended as follows: Articles 2(11), 2(12), 6, 8, 9, 13, 14, and 16 to 18, are deleted as of [date of entry into force].

**Justification**

Preserving Articles 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 11 and 12 of the P2B Regulation is essential to ensure fair and balanced platform-to-business relations. National enforcement demonstrates their practical value. These provisions safeguard transparency, protect businesses from sudden loss of market access, preserve pricing freedom, and ensure access to effective, low-cost dispute resolution. Neither the DSA nor the DMA provides equivalent protection: the DSA focuses on consumer-facing obligations, while the DMA applies only to designated gatekeepers. Removing these provisions would create a regulatory gap, particularly for SMEs that depend on online platforms.
